📚 ⭐ 🗺️ Hi, explorer! I'm your reading buddy. Together we will find, hear and read amazing words. Are you ready?

Needs internet. Reading aloud needs Chrome or Edge and microphone permission. Your voice is never recorded or saved.

How to play

  1. Pick your level.
  2. Press PLAY WORD to hear the word.
  3. Press MICROPHONE and read the word aloud.
  4. See your result, then press NEXT WORD.
  5. Finish 10 words to earn stars!

Missed a word? That's okay – practice helps you grow. If the microphone doesn't work, ask your teacher to listen.
